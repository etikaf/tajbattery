About us

Batteries, UPS and solar in Pehlwan Goth since 2012

Taj Battery Service opened in Pehlwan Goth in 2012. Since then, families and shopkeepers have come to us for batteries, UPS units, charging and repairs, and for solar systems. We design and install complete solar systems for homes, shops and offices across Karachi.

When you buy solar from us, you deal with a shop you can walk into. If something needs attention later, you know where to find us.

  • Car and UPS batteries from Osaka, Exide, Phoenix and AGS
  • UPS and inverter repair at our own bench
  • Solar panels, inverters, cables and accessories
  • Complete solar installation (net metering coming soon)
