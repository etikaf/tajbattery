Installation

How a solar installation works

A typical 5 kW home system is installed in one to two days after the order is confirmed.

  1. Site visit and load check

    We visit your home or shop, check the roof, shading and direction, and list the appliances you want to run.

  2. System design and quote

    You get a written quote with panel count, inverter, battery and structure, at the day's price.

  3. Installation

    Our team fits the structure, panels, inverter, battery, wiring, breakers, surge protection and earthing.

  4. Testing and handover

    We test the system, set up monitoring on your phone and show you how to use it.

  5. Ready for net metering

    Your system is set up so it can be net metered later. Our net metering application service is coming soon.

  6. After-sales support

    Call or WhatsApp us for faults, cleaning, upgrades or adding batteries later.

K-Electric

Net metering in Karachi Coming soon

With net metering, extra units your panels produce in the day go to K-Electric and are adjusted against your bill. We are getting ready to handle net metering applications. Here is how it will work.

What you need

  • Copy of CNIC of the meter owner
  • Latest K-Electric bill
  • Proof of ownership or tenancy of the property
  • An installed on-grid or hybrid system

What we will do

  • Prepare the application and system drawings
  • Submit the file and follow up with K-Electric
  • Coordinate the inspection
  • Stay with you until the green meter is fitted

Net metering rules and buy-back rates are set by NEPRA and can change. Ask us about the current rules before you decide.
