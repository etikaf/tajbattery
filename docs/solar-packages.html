System types

On-grid, hybrid or off-grid?

Sizing

Size a system from your bill

Take the units from your K-Electric bill and divide by about 125. That gives the system size in kW.

Monthly units on your billSuggested systemTypical home
Up to 3503 kWFans, lights, fridge, no AC
